Privacy Policy

YLNEV Studios operates Spell & Simmer, a general-audience puzzle game. Contact: Ylnevstudios@gmail.com.

1. GAME PROGRESS AND ACCOUNTS

The game does not require an account. Progress, coins, hearts, pantry collections, preferences, and legal acceptance are saved locally on your device. The game does not upload these save files to a YLNEV Studios server. Removing the app can remove its local save data; operating-system backups are controlled by your device settings.

2. ADVERTISING AND THIRD-PARTY DATA

Spell & Simmer uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to show interstitial ads between some completed levels and optional rewarded ads for in-game benefits. It also uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage applicable consent and privacy choices. There are no real-money coin purchases.

Google's advertising SDK may process IP addresses (including approximate location inferred from an IP address), app- or device-related identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, app interactions, crash information, performance measurements, and other diagnostic information. Google uses information to deliver and measure advertising, prevent fraud, provide analytics, and maintain and improve its services. Data may be linked to app/device identifiers even though the game does not ask for your name or account.

The iOS release requests non-personalized ads, disables AdMob's publisher first-party ID, and does not request App Tracking Transparency permission or access to the advertising identifier (IDFA). Non-personalized advertising still involves data processing, including ad measurement, contextual selection, and fraud prevention; it does not mean that no data is collected. Regional consent requirements still apply. Other platforms will be disclosed and configured before their release.

3. CONSENT AND CHOICES

Where required, the game requests privacy choices through Google UMP before loading ads. A Privacy Choices option is available in the game settings when Google indicates that it is required. You can use it to review or change applicable choices. Ad availability can vary with consent, network availability, and region. You can play the core puzzles without accepting optional rewarded ads.

4. SHARING AND SERVICE PROVIDERS

Google and advertising partners process advertising and diagnostic information under their own policies and applicable consent choices. Advertising information may be shared with entities that deliver or measure ads. YLNEV Studios does not operate a separate advertising-data backend or sell local game-save files. The legal meaning of sharing or selling information can vary by jurisdiction; applicable choices are presented through the consent interface.

Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
How Google uses information from apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
Google advertising controls: https://myadcenter.google.com/
Google SDK data disclosures: https://developers.google.com/admob/ios/privacy/data-disclosure

5. SUPPORT

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, message, and any screenshots or diagnostics you attach. We use it to answer your request and troubleshoot problems. Do not send passwords or other unnecessary sensitive information. Contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any necessary legal retention.

6. CHILDREN

Spell & Simmer is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly ask children to provide personal information. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, contact Ylnevstudios@gmail.com so we can address it. Advertising content is subject to the ad provider's controls and the app's configured content restrictions.

7. RETENTION, SECURITY, AND INTERNATIONAL PROCESSING

Local game data stays on your device subject to your actions and platform backup settings. Google controls retention of information processed by its services under its policies. Data handled by third-party services may be processed in countries outside your own. Contact Google through its privacy controls for rights relating to data it controls, and contact us for support information we hold. We use reasonable safeguards, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. YOUR RIGHTS AND CHANGES

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of personal information, or withdraw consent. Use the in-game privacy choices where available and the provider links above, or contact us for help identifying the appropriate service. We update this policy when practices change and revise the date above. Material changes may require additional notice or consent.
