How can we help?

For help with Spell & Simmer, bug reports, or privacy questions, email Ylnevstudios@gmail.com.

Report a problem

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, game version, the level or screen affected, and what happened. A screenshot can help. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary personal information.

Game progress

Progress is stored on your device. Avoid deleting the app while troubleshooting: uninstalling can remove your saved progress.

Ads and rewards

Rewarded ads require an available ad and a network connection. A reward is granted when the ad provider confirms completion. If an ad is unavailable, try again later or use earned coins where offered.

Privacy choices

When required by the consent provider, Privacy Choices is available in the game settings. Read the Privacy Policy for information about advertising and data use.
